📅 Day 1 Action Plan - Eaglepoint.ai

Morning - Before 10 AM

1. Get Aquila Admin Access

Walk in calm and curious: "I want to make sure I'm not adding reporting burden to the team, so I'd like to understand what data I can pull from Aquila directly. Can someone walk me through what's visible on the admin side?"

Morning - 10:30 AM

2. Send GM Signal (5-minute DM)

Message to GM: "Got Aquila access. Want to understand the priority - should I focus on: (a) identifying who's stuck and why, (b) getting the tracking system in place, or (c) first impressions on team dynamics? Want to make sure I'm working on what matters most to you."

This does two things: shows initiative and gets alignment.

Mid-morning - 11 AM

3. Observe Team in Action

Don't talk to developers yet. Watch: How do they work? What's the vibe? Who's talking to whom? Where do they sit? This is reconnaissance, not intervention.

Lunch - 12:30 PM

4. Casual 1-on-1s (Optional)

Join a developer for lunch if appropriate. Ask: "What are you working on that's interesting? Anything you wish was easier?" No agenda. Just listen.

Afternoon - 2 PM

5. Send First Check-ins (if appropriate)

Start with 1-2 developers who seem most approachable. Not a formal meeting - just a Slack DM: "Hey [name], I'm [your name]. Just getting oriented this week - happy to help with anything that's slowing you down. Let me know."

End of Day - 5 PM

6. Document Everything

Write in your tracker: Who did you talk to? What did you learn about their work? What's the infrastructure situation? What's the team vibe? This becomes your baseline.
